A calculator inspired by graphing calculators like the NumWorks, named after the mechanical calculator Blaise Pascal built in 1642.

Calculator
• Natural input: powers, fractions, roots and integral bounds in boxes, as on paper
• Exact answers next to approximations: √(−5) = √5i ≈ 2.236i, Σ 1/n² = π²/6
• Symbolic derivatives and antiderivatives, with integration by parts and partial fractions
• Definite integrals, sums and products, including infinite ones
• Simplification, expansion and factorization of polynomials with their roots
• Complex numbers on a complex plane
• Units and constants, shown in the easiest unit to read
• Decimal, hexadecimal, binary and prime factors of integers

Functions
• Expressions, Graph and Table pages
• Several functions in their own colors, zoom one axis at a time

Probability
• Ten laws: binomial, uniform, exponential, normal, chi-squared, Student, geometric, Poisson, Fisher and hypergeometric
• Type a, b or the probability right inside P(X ≤ a) = p
• Expected value and variance of every law, with the formula, your values and why it holds
• Binomial explained with probability trees and Pascal's triangle

Sequences
• As many explicit or recursive sequences as you want, with a graph and a table

Statistics and regression
• Histogram, box plot, quartiles, mean and standard deviation
• Eight kinds of regression curves with r and r²

Inference
• Tests and confidence intervals for proportions and means, and the χ² goodness of fit test

Finance
• Simple and compound interest: find any of N, I%, PV, Pmt and FV

Elements
• Periodic table of the 118 elements with their main properties

Price comparison
• Compare offers by price per kg, L, m or piece, even across units

And also
• Unit converter with watt-hours, date calculator, time zones and body mass index
• Reorder and hide the apps in the menu
• Phone keypad layout, a static cursor, and the app stays open on the lock screen

No internet permission: everything is computed on the device.
