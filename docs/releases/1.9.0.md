Pascaline 1.9.0

### New tool
- **Geometry**: every shape drawn to scale, with its results and formulas
  - **Triangle**: any three values with at least one side solve it, by the law of sines or of cosines. When two sides and an angle fit two triangles, both can be picked. Area, perimeter, heights, inradius, circumradius, and whether it is acute, right or obtuse, scalene, isosceles or equilateral
  - **Quadrilaterals**: square, rectangle, parallelogram, rhombus, trapezoid and kite, with area, perimeter and diagonals
  - **Circle**: from its radius, diameter, circumference or area, and a sector of it with its arc, chord and segment
  - **3D solids**: cube, cuboid, sphere, cylinder, cone and square pyramid, with volume, surface, lateral area and slant height

### Gears
- Each gear can mesh, share a shaft, be a ring around the gear before it, run on a belt or chain, be turned by a worm or push a rack
- Give the motor, or what the output needs and get the motor
- Checks on every mesh: contact ratio, tooth speed and tooth strength with a safety factor, with warnings
- **Planetary**: sun, planets and ring, with one held and one turned, animated
- **Find teeth**: the gear trains that give a wanted ratio
- Examples, and trains saved under a name

### Currency changeover
- The answer comes first: what the amount is worth today with inflation, and the same money at the fixed rate of each changeover
- A timeline to pick the money and the year: tap a currency, or tap and drag along the years
- **Then and now**: the amount's share of a month's take-home pay, the hours of work it took, what a loaf cost and how many it bought, and how food rose against all prices

### Smaller changes
- Deleting a history card is one smooth movement
- Sky and Earth says the sun is "56° below the horizon", instead of an angle that looked like a temperature
