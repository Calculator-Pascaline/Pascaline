Pascaline 1.8.0

### New tools
- **Gears**: a gear train drawn turning, with meshing gears and gears on the same shaft. Speed, torque, power and center distance through every gear, from the module or the diametral pitch, with the efficiency of each mesh
- **Inflation**: what an amount from one year is worth in another, on a price curve you drag, with the inflation of each year. Euro, US dollar since 1913, pound, yen, Swiss franc, Canadian and Australian dollars and yuan
- **Currency changeover**: the old money of 12 countries through each changeover at its legal rate (old franc, Deutsche Mark, lira, peseta, pounds shillings and pence…) and what it is worth today
  - **Then and now**: the price of a loaf and the average monthly pay at the time, and what the amount meant compared with them
- **3D graphs**: z = f(x, y) that you turn with one finger and zoom with two, or seen from above as level curves. Maxima, minima and saddle points are found and marked
- **Vector fields** is gone: the value, gradient and Laplacian at a point are now in 3D graphs

### Calculator
- **Unit key**: constants are sorted into five groups, with five new ones: ħ, the atomic mass constant, the Bohr radius, the molar volume and the Stefan–Boltzmann constant, all at their CODATA 2022 values
- More units: inductance, pF, nF, MΩ, areas and volumes in metric and imperial, kPa, MPa, mmHg, psi, pound-force and calories
- Speed only offers knots: write m/s or km/h as a length over a time. Old history using them still works
- **Integrals**: the "…" of an integral solved by parts shows its DI table, and how the method works
- Results below 1 keep 3 significant digits: 0.00345 stays 0.00345
- Backspace can remove a fraction bar with nothing on top

### Unit converter
- Hours can be typed as hours and minutes, like feet and inches ([Unitto #193](https://github.com/sadellie/unitto/issues/193) by [testycaracal](https://github.com/testycaracal))
- The unit list no longer leaves results invisible while you type a search ([Unitto #303](https://github.com/sadellie/unitto/issues/303) by [RamPawar7](https://github.com/RamPawar7))
- Favorite units stay first in the list while a value is typed ([Unitto #313](https://github.com/sadellie/unitto/issues/313) by [lammel-hub](https://github.com/lammel-hub))

### Settings
- The apostrophe as thousands separator: 1'234'567 ([Unitto #302](https://github.com/sadellie/unitto/issues/302) by [BenutzerX](https://github.com/BenutzerX))
- A switch to put backspace before the decimal key on the bottom row ([Unitto #288](https://github.com/sadellie/unitto/issues/288) by [sud0xenn](https://github.com/sud0xenn))
- The licenses screen lists every library the app uses ([Unitto #252](https://github.com/sadellie/unitto/issues/252) by [sadellie](https://github.com/sadellie))

### Home
- The app opens on the tool you used last
- Tiles grow into their tool and back, and Home comes back as you left it

### Languages
- 9 new languages: Arabic, Bengali, Czech, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Slovak, Ukrainian and Vietnamese, 22 in all. Czech and Slovak ([Unitto #325](https://github.com/sadellie/unitto/issues/325) by [janojakubik](https://github.com/janojakubik))
- Arabic reads from right to left, while keypads and math stay left to right

### Good to know
- Unitto's name and code are now Pascaline's everywhere. Your history, units and settings stay, but **widgets and the quick settings tile have to be added again** once
- The README gives the SHA-256 fingerprint of the signing certificate, so you can check that your copy is the real one ([Unitto #314](https://github.com/sadellie/unitto/issues/314) by [TheJNXx](https://github.com/TheJNXx) and [Unitto #316](https://github.com/sadellie/unitto/issues/316) by [PeonyThePeon](https://github.com/PeonyThePeon))
